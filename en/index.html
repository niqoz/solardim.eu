Three systems, one tool

Each study is filed under its client, compared with its variants and exported as a PDF report.

Off-grid site

Panels, battery and backup generator for a site with no grid, tested over 18 weather years: the report keeps an unfavourable year.

Self-consumption

Matches hourly production, shading included, with a consumption profile: water heater, electric car, battery, export and payback.

Solar water heating

Sizes a solar water heater and compares the backup with an electric water heater, month by month.

On the roof, your phone

  • surveys the real horizon: trees, buildings, terrain.
  • measures the roof’s tilt and orientation.
  • Works offline, with no signal.
  • One shading mask per roof face, aligned on the sun.
  • Export to PVGIS and PVsyst.

The client report

A clear PDF report, to print or to send to a phone.

  • Europe, with the tariffs and CO₂ factor of each grid zone.
  • Production, self-consumption, conservative twenty-year payback.

From the phone to the office

A study started on site reopens on the Windows computer, and the other way round. Same calculation, same report, folders and backups shared by both.
