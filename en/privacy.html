Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This translation is provided for convenience; the French version prevails.

SolarDim is a solar design application for installers, available on Android and Windows. This page explains what data it processes, where that data goes and how you stay in control of it.

In short: SolarDim has no user account, and your studies never leave your device. Your client folders, surveys and photos stay with you. Only two things are sent out: a site’s coordinates, to public calculation and mapping services; and, for the subscription, a randomly generated device identifier, to our licence server. We know neither your name nor your email address, nor those of your clients.

What stays on your device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your studies and their settings;
  • the client folders you enter: name, address, phone number, email address, notes;
  • solar shading mask surveys and roof orientations;
  • site photos, resized and saved again without their metadata (GPS position, device);
  • your installer profile (company name, contact details, logo), which appears on the PDF reports you hand to your clients.

Your clients’ data is entrusted to you, the professional: you are the controller within the meaning of the GDPR. SolarDim never has access to it.

We use no advertising, no audience measurement and no automatic crash reporting.

The subscription and its licence

SolarDim works on a subscription, taken out in Google Play from the Android application. The Windows client is activated with the phone’s subscription, using a pairing code displayed on the phone.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We see neither your payment method, nor your name, nor your email address; Google processes them under its own privacy policy.

The server returns a signed licence to each device, which the application checks offline: a site with no signal does not block your work. It is renewed once a day when a connection is available.

Retention periods on the server:

  • the IP address used to count attempts: ten minutes;
  • a pairing code: ten minutes, or until it is used;
  • the licence and its devices: for as long as the subscription exists, then one year after it ends, so that your devices can be found again if you resubscribe. They are then deleted automatically.

Legal basis: performance of the subscription contract. Without this data, we could not give you access to what you have paid for.

End of subscription: your studies and folders remain on your device, accessible and editable; only calculation, PDF reports and exports to PVGIS and PVsyst are then reserved for subscribers.

The PDF reports and study files you export or share go wherever you send them: the application and the recipient you choose.

Permissions requested on Android

  • Camera: survey the solar shading mask and photograph the site.
  • Location: place the site on the map and align the survey on the sun’s position. Approximate location is enough for the compass.
  • Internet: query the services listed above.

Motion sensors (orientation, compass) require no permission; their readings stay on the device.

Backups

Android backup to your Google account: your client folders, your current study and your site photos are excluded. Only your settings and your installer profile are included. On Android 11 and earlier, as the system does not distinguish cloud backup from device transfer, folders are not backed up by Android at all; the application tells you so.

Transfer to a new phone: it also copies your folders and photos, directly from one device to the other.

The device licence is never backed up or transferred: a new phone is activated again, and your folders follow it normally.

SolarDim backup: the file you create from the application contains all your folders; you choose where it is saved.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can delete a study or a folder from the application; uninstalling the application erases all its data from the device. Cached maps and calculation results are purged automatically. Licence server data follows the periods given above.

Your rights

Your study data is only on your device: your rights of access, rectification and erasure are exercised there directly. For licence server data, write to contact@solardim.eu including the Google Play order number of your subscription: without an account, it is what allows us to find your licence. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
